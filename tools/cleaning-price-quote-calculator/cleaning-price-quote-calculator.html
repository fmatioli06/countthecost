Settings & assumptions

Cleaning pace

Square feet one cleaner covers in an hour, for a standard clean. These are starting estimates — tune them to match your own jobs. They feed the three choices in the Cleaning pace dropdown.

Service time multipliers

How much longer each service takes versus a standard clean. A standard clean is the baseline at 1.0.
1.0 · baseline

Condition time multipliers

How much the home's condition stretches the time. Average is the baseline at 1.0.
1.0 · baseline

How the price is calculated

In square-footage mode, the tool estimates the time, then prices it for profit. Step by step:

1. Hours. square feet ÷ cleaning pace gives the cleaner-hours for a standard clean. That's multiplied by the service and condition numbers above. Only square footage feeds this — bedrooms and bathrooms are quote details. More cleaners finishes the same work faster, so it lowers on-site time, not total labor.

2. Labor. cleaner-hours × pay per hour. If you're cleaning it yourself, your wage counts here too.

3. Your cost. labor + supplies + travel + overhead, plus the cost of any add-ons and the card fee.

4. Price. cost ÷ (1 − margin − card fee) marks the standard clean up so you still hit your target margin after the processor takes its cut. Add-on charges are added on top, then discount, minimum, and tax.

These assumptions only apply in square-footage mode. In "Enter manually" mode you set the hours yourself, so service and condition just describe the job on the quote.
